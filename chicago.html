Welcome to Chicago

Chicago is the largest city in Illinois and one of the most vibrant cities in the United States. Known for its iconic skyline, world-class museums, and deep-dish pizza, Chicago attracts millions of visitors each year.

Quick Facts

  • Incorporated: March 4, 1837
    • Though incorporated in 1837, much of Chicago burnt down during the Great Chicago Fire of 1871
    • Much of the city is more modern than similarly aged cities in the US
  • Region: Northeastern Illinois, along Lake Michigan
  • Classification: Urban
  • The third most populace city in the US after New York and Los Angeles

Must-See Attractions

  1. Art Institute of Chicago
    • Explore one of the oldest and largest art museums in the U.S.
  2. Skydeck Chicago
    • Experience bone chilling views from the Willis Tower's observation deck.
      * Locals will still call it the Sears Tower
  3. Navy Pier
    • Enjoy waterfront dining, entertainment, and iconic rides at this family-friendly destination.
