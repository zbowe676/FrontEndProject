Illinois: A State of History, Culture, and Adventure

Illinois, one of the greatest states in the union, is home to the third-largest city, Chicago. It offers a vibrant blend of culture, history, and attractions that captivate visitors.

Immerse yourself in Illinois’ captivating Civil War history at its many museums and historical sites. Or, take in breathtaking views from one of the country’s tallest buildings. And for a taste of the past, visit the ancient remains of Cahokia Mounds, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Whether you’re looking for urban excitement or serene natural beauty, Illinois has something for everyone.

Things to Do in Illinois (Outside of Chicago, Schaumburg, and Springfield)

Top 3 Attractions

  1. Visit Starved Rock
    • Enjoy scenic hiking trails, rent a canoe, and enjoy fishing in beautiful scenery.
  2. Tour the Cahokia Mounds
    • Explore the awe-inspiring remnants of one of Pre-Columbian America's largest cities.
  3. Six Flags: Great America
    • Experience exhilarating rides, family-friendly attractions, and unforgettable entertainment at Illinois' premier amusement park.
