Schaumburg

Where city meets suburbia

Welcome to Schamburg, Illinois

Schaumburg is a bustling suburban city located northwest of Chicago. Known for its shopping centers, vibrant business environment, and family-friendly amenities, Schaumburg offers something for everyone.

Quick Facts

  • Schaumburg was incorporated in 1956.
  • It is located in the northeastern region of Illinois.
  • Schaumburg is classified as a suburban city.

Must-See Attractions

  1. Woodfield Mall
    • One of the largest shopping destinations in the U.S., featuring hundreds of stores and dining options.
  2. LEGOLAND Discovery Center
    • A family-friendly attraction featuring LEGO-themed rides, workshops, and play areas.
  3. Mitsuwa Marketplace
    • Experience a taste of Japan at Mitsuwa Marketplace in Chicago. Shop for authentic Japanese groceries, explore a variety of anime and manga, and savor delicious Japanese cuisine in the vibrant food court. A perfect destination for a cultural day trip!
