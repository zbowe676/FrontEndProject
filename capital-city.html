Springfield, Illinois:

The Capital City

Springfield is the capital of Illinois and is renowned for its rich historical significance. As the home of President Abraham Lincoln, the city continues to draw visitors eager to explore its historical sites and landmarks.

Quick Facts

  • Springfield is located in central Illinois
  • Springfield was founded on April 10, 1821. It was incorporated as a town on April 2, 1832, and later received its city charter on February 3, 1840.
  • Due to the population size, dense population, and extensive infrastructure, Springfield is considered an Urban City

Need to See!

Take a moment to go back in time and visit Abraham Lincoln's home before he became the 16th President of the United States.

Visit Illinois Old State Capitol in Springfield which served as the state's government center from 1839 to 1876. Within these walls, state legislator, Abraham Lincoln, debated key issues of his time. The building also witnessed his famous "House Divided" speech in 1858. Today, the beautifully restored capitol stands as a monument to Lincoln's legacy and a symbol of American democracy. Visitors can explore its chambers and gain insight into the political landscape that shaped the nation's history
